# Neon Orbit Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 5, 2026

Neon Orbit is an offline arcade game. This build does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information.

## Data stored on the device

The game stores only local preferences needed for gameplay:

- Best score
- Sound setting
- Haptic feedback setting
- Reduced-effects setting

These values are stored in Android app preferences. They may be included in the user's Android backup or device-to-device transfer if the user has enabled those platform features.

## Network access

The app does not request Internet permission and contains no advertising, analytics, account, social, or cloud SDK.

## Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other user.

## Changes

If a future release adds online services, advertising, analytics, accounts, purchases, or other data processing, this policy and the Play Console Data safety declaration must be updated before release.
