# Google Play Store Listing Draft

## App name
Neon Orbit

## Short description
One tap. Two orbits. Dodge the signal, charge OVERLOAD, chase your best run.

## Full description
Neon Orbit is a fast, minimalist arcade challenge built around a single decision: inner orbit or outer orbit.

Tap anywhere to switch lanes. Slip past incoming signal hazards, collect energy shards, and fill the core to trigger OVERLOAD — a short burst of power that lets you smash straight through danger.

Every run accelerates. Every missed shard breaks your rhythm. Every second asks for one more clean decision.

FEATURES
• One-tap controls with instant restart
• Smooth procedural neon graphics and particles
• Dynamic difficulty that ramps with your run
• OVERLOAD power state and combo scoring
• Local high score
• Sound and haptic feedback controls
• Reduced-effects accessibility option
• Fully offline play — no account required

No ads. No login. No Internet connection required in this build.

## Suggested category
Games → Arcade

## Content notes
Abstract geometric hazards only; no realistic violence, gambling, chat, user-generated content, or location features.
